Privacy Policy for ChromaBounce

LAST UPDATED: MAY 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChromaBounce ("the App") handles information. This document serves as a clear statement of data practices, engineered to protect both the user and the integrity of the application platform.

1. Absolute Zero-Data Architecture

  • No Collection: The App does not collect, harvest, aggregate, or intercept any personal information, usage metrics, diagnostic logs, or device-specific identifiers.
  • Local Processing: All gameplay data, high scores, sector progression matrices, and user configurations are stored natively on your local device's secure storage and within an isolated, localized database queue[cite: 3, 4].
  • Zero Transmission: No data is ever transmitted, leaked, or synced to external servers or remote infrastructures.

2. App Store Privacy Label Compliance

In strict accordance with global app marketplace transparency mandates, the official data disclosure labels for the App are defined as follows:

  • Data Not Collected: The App does not collect any data.
  • No Tracking: The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • No Data Linked to You: No data is compiled or associated with your identity.

3. Global Regulatory Alignments

Because the App operates with an absolute zero-data collection footprint, it automatically complies with major international privacy frameworks by eliminating the risk of data exposure:

  • Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & GDPR-K: The App does not collect, request, or process any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable jurisdictional age limits). It is entirely safe for all audiences.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & U.S. State Privacy Laws: Because zero personal data is collected or maintained, there is no data payload to access, review, port, delete, or opt out of.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): The App establishes zero data processing pipelines, meaning no personal data processing, tracking, or profiling takes place.

4. Third-Party Services & Boundaries

The App is built using Apple's native iOS framework architectures. While the App itself contains zero data tracking, players may optionally interact with native system-level features provided by Apple Inc. (such as Game Center cloud save authentication or iCloud backup services).

Any such interactions are governed exclusively by your device-level system settings and Apple’s standard Privacy Policy. The App does not have access to individual platform credentials or account tokens generated by these system services.

5. Indemnification & Limitation of Liability

  • As-Is Provision: The App is distributed as-is, with no implied warranties regarding uninterrupted or error-free execution.
  • Mutual Protection: Because no data is collected, stored, or managed on external infrastructure by the App, there is no liability for local hardware failures, unauthorized physical device access, or data corruption occurring on the user's personal hardware.
